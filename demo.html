Interactive Spot Guide

CRITICAL EXPOSURE

Click hotspots on the screen above

1. Suspicious Email (Monitor)

This email contains a phishing link mimicking an urgent company HR announcement. Never click links from unknown or suspicious senders. Report to IT immediately.

2. Weak Password (Sticky Note)

Password written on a physical sticky note! Never write passwords down on desks, displays, or keyboards. Use a secure company password manager instead.

3. Sensitive Information on Screen

Sensitive company information is visible on the monitor. Always lock your screen when leaving your workstation and avoid exposing confidential information.

4. Unsecured Mobile Device

A mobile device containing sensitive information has been left unsecured. Keep company devices protected and never leave them unattended.

5. Unauthorized Visitor Risk

An unattended area near the workstation can provide unauthorized people access to the office environment. Keep restricted areas secure at all times.

6. Unlocked Workstation

The workstation is accessible without proper protection. Always lock your computer before stepping away from your desk.
